BuildEra Privacy Policy
Tanggal Efektif: 27 Oktober 2026 | Developer: Solutivo Labs Indonesia
1. Introduction
BuildEra is a point-of-sale (POS) and business management application developed by Solutivo Labs Indonesia. It is designed to help businesses manage sales, inventory, purchases, suppliers, receivables, and business reports.

This Privacy Policy explains how BuildEra handles information when you use the application, including information stored locally on your device and information processed through supported cloud services.

By using BuildEra, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Handle
Depending on the features you use, BuildEra may process the following categories of information:

A. Business and Transaction Data
Product names, descriptions, prices, stock quantities, and units of measurement.
Sales transactions, purchase records, and payment information.
Supplier details and business contact information.
Customer records and outstanding receivables.
Business reports and related operational data.
B. Account Information
If you use account-based features, BuildEra may process information associated with your account, such as your email address and authentication-related identifiers.

C. Images and Files
BuildEra may allow you to select images or files for business purposes, including product photos, business logos, and QRIS payment images.

D. Technical and Synchronization Data
When cloud synchronization is enabled, the application may process information required to synchronize business records, maintain synchronization status, and identify pending changes.

3. How Information Is Stored
BuildEra is designed with an offline-first approach.

Local storage: Business information may be stored on your device so that supported features remain usable without an internet connection.

Cloud synchronization: When cloud features are configured and enabled, supported information may be transmitted to cloud infrastructure to synchronize data between supported application sessions or devices.

4. How We Use Information
Provide sales, inventory, purchasing, and business management functionality.
Maintain business records and generate reports.
Authenticate users and support account-related features.
Synchronize supported data between local storage and cloud services.
Troubleshoot technical problems and improve reliability.
Protect the application and its data against unauthorized access or misuse.
5. Third-Party Services
BuildEra may use third-party infrastructure and services including:

Supabase: Authentication, database storage, and related backend functionality (Supabase Privacy).
Google Play services: Application distribution and platform-related functionality (Google Privacy).
6. Data Sharing
Solutivo Labs Indonesia does not sell users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers when necessary to operate features that you use or as required by law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion
Information stored locally remains on your device until deleted or uninstalled. Information synchronized to cloud services remains stored until deleted through supported functionality. For deletion assistance, contact us at solutivolabs@gmail.com.

8. Data Security
We use appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by BuildEra. Users are responsible for maintaining device and account security.

9. Children's Privacy
BuildEra is designed for business and operational use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Subscriptions and Payments
A. Payment Processing
Subscriptions purchased through the Google Play Store are processed by Google Play in accordance with Google Play Terms of Service and Privacy Policies.

B. Subscription Information
BuildEra processes subscription status, entitlement eligibility, plan identifiers, and purchase verification references to grant access to Pro features.

C. Renewals, Cancellations, and Refunds
Subscriptions can be managed or canceled anytime via your Google Play Account settings (Google Play Subscriptions).

11. Subscription Plans and Feature Access
A. Free Plan
The Free plan provides limited core POS functionality and is subject to a limit of 3 sales transactions. It does not include Excel product import, business analytics, receivables, or purchasing functionality.

B. Pro Subscription
The Pro subscription unlocks full features including unlimited product management, unlimited sales transactions, purchasing management, billing, accounts receivable, business analytics, and Excel product import.

C. Subscription Pricing
Monthly Pro: IDR 99,000 per month (excl. applicable taxes).
Annual Pro: IDR 999,000 per year (excl. applicable taxes — save ~15.9%).
