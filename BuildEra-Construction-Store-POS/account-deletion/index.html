Account deletion guide

This page explains how BuildEra users can request deletion of their account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion, contact the BuildEra support team using the support email address provided in the app's Settings or Help & Support section.

Use the subject line Account Deletion Request and include:

  • The email address registered to your BuildEra account.
  • A clear statement that you want your BuildEra account deleted.
  • Your store or business name, if applicable, to help us identify the account.
For your security, the support team may ask you to confirm the request from the email address registered to your BuildEra account before processing deletion.

What happens after you submit a request?

1 Request verification

We verify that the request is authorized by the account owner.

2 Data deletion

After verification, the account and eligible associated data are deleted from our systems.

3 Confirmation

We send a confirmation when the deletion process has been completed.

Data that may be deleted

Depending on the features you used, deletion may include:

  • Your BuildEra account and authentication profile.
  • Store data, such as products, product units, customers, and suppliers.
  • Business records, such as transactions, transaction items, purchases, and payment records.
  • Related uploaded product images and other account-associated content.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law, regulation, dispute resolution, or legitimate security and accounting requirements. If any data must be retained, it will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

Important information

  • Google Play subscriptions: Deleting your BuildEra account does not automatically cancel a subscription or stop future billing. Manage or cancel your subscription separately through Google Play.
  • Offline data: Some information may remain in the app's local storage on a device that is offline. After deletion is confirmed, sign out and clear the app's local data or uninstall BuildEra from your device.
  • Deletion scope: Account deletion removes eligible data associated with the account from BuildEra's systems. It does not delete information independently stored by third-party services or information that must legally be retained.

Need help with your request?

Contact BuildEra support through the email address shown in the app.
